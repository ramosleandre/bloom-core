# Technical experts (EXP)

> Complementary objective C of the Epitech follow-up: have outside experts challenge our choices, and show what that changed. Experts' contact details and the details of the exchanges are not published here; this file keeps the topics, the decisions and their trace in the code.

## 1. Topics to have challenged (draft, before end of December)

| # | Topic | What we are not sure about | Options still open | Specific questions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Choice and evaluation of the decision model | is a hosted model worth the loss of privacy? is a set of 50 situations enough to tell two backends apart? | hosted System 1 · local System 1 · rules | What set size is needed to separate two backends within 5 points? How do we measure the calibration of probabilities? |
| 2 | Defense against indirect injection in an action loop on the user's machine | is the trust label on fragments enough as a safeguard? | systematic approval · `UNTRUSTED` labels · isolated execution | Which attacks does our policy let through? |
| 3 | Contextual pie menu and muscle memory | does reordering the actions on every opening break gesture learning? | fixed positions · contextual slots · hybrid | How many contextual slots before losing the advantage of a radial menu? |
